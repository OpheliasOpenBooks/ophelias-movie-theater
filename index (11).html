A Century of Silver-Screen Magic

From silent pictures to the birth of sound, early cinema shaped the way we tell stories. Our future cinema-history exhibits will explore the artists, innovations, and audiences behind these remarkable films.

Our Mission

Ophelia's Movie Theater celebrates the art of classic cinema by sharing carefully researched public-domain films, free for everyone to enjoy. We believe yesterday's stories deserve a place in tomorrow's world.
